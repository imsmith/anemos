    Anemos.Runtime.NLD (anemos v0.1.0)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Natural Language Date resolver.

    Resolves NLD_<text> expressions to NaiveDateTime values. All times are system-local.

    Supported expressions

    ExpressionResolves to
    tomorrowNext day at 00:00:01
    yesterdayPrevious day at 00:00:01
    todayCurrent day at 00:00:01
    next.monday .. next.sundayNext occurrence of that day at 00:00:01
    next.weekNext monday at 00:00:01
    next.monthFirst of next month at 00:00:01
    next.yearJanuary 1 of next year at 00:00:01
    end.of.monthLast day of current month at 23:59:59
    end.of.weekNext sunday at 23:59:59

    Examples

    iex> {:ok, dt} = Anemos.Runtime.NLD.resolve("today")
iex> dt.hour
0

iex> {:error, %Comn.Errors.ErrorStruct{code: "anemos.nld/unknown_expression"}} =
...>   Anemos.Runtime.NLD.resolve("gobbledygook")
